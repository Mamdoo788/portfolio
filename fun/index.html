02

Market Weather ⛅

The market has four moods. My robots dress for the weather. Tap a cloud — the whole page changes colour, and the octopus's tentacles change length. 🎨

☀️Sunny Everything up and to the right. Wear shorts, buy things. size ×1.00 · full send
⛅Meh Nothing is happening. Nobody knows anything. size ×0.50 · half a send
⛈️Storm Going down, and jumpy about it. Buying here loses money — I checked. 💸 size ×0.25 · barely a send
🌫️Foggy Genuinely cannot tell. So we sit still and touch nothing. size ×0.60 · HOLD, do not fiddle

Fog is the important one. 🌫️ A robot that always has an answer will confidently give you a rubbish answer on the days when there isn't one — and a confident rubbish answer looks exactly like a good one until the money leaves. So it shrugs instead. Shrugging is a feature. 🤷

03

The Dead Flower Problem 🥀

My biggest, most embarrassing, most important discovery. It's about gardening. Sort of.

flowers I planted 309 companies that were in the club at some point
still alive today ~233 the other 76 got bought, kicked out, or went bust 💀

Here's the trap: if you test your strategy only on the flowers that are still alive, you've quietly deleted every disaster from history. Your results look amazing for a reason that has nothing to do with you.

I did this. For ages. Then I found it, dug up the whole garden, replanted it properly with the dead ones included, and my beautiful numbers got much uglier. 📉 Good.
